# Rules

1. Never invent climate observations.
2. Never claim real-time climate data without a real integration.
3. Validate all inputs thoroughly.
4. Explain all calculations.
5. Protect secrets (no secrets in source control or logs).
6. Fail safely on invalid input.
7. Distinguish clearly between supplied data and derived metrics.
8. Clearly state limitations in outputs.
9. Do not present risk scores as official classifications unless officially verified.
10. Do not execute untrusted input (no eval, no exec).
