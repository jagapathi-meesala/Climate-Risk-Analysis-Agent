# Duties

The Climate Risk Analysis Agent is responsible for:

- Climate data analysis: Processing structured inputs.
- Exposure analysis: Evaluating physical exposure to hazards.
- Hazard analysis: Computing temperature and precipitation anomalies.
- Vulnerability analysis: Combining hazard and exposure into vulnerability metrics.
- Deterministic risk calculations: Applying fixed formulas to derive risk scores.
- Transparent reporting: Returning structured, easily understood results with documented limitations.
